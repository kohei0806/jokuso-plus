2. App purpose and target audience

JOKUSO+ is a wound-progress recording app for visiting nurses and care-facility nurses in Japan, used to document and visualize pressure ulcers ("jokuso" in Japanese) according to DESIGN-R®, a standard Japanese clinical scoring tool for pressure ulcer severity. It solves the problem of tracking a patient's wound over multiple visits on paper: the nurse selects the relevant severity option for each of the tool's seven categories (Depth, Exudate, Size, Inflammation/Infection, Granulation tissue, Necrotic tissue, Pocket), the app automatically calculates the total score and a shorthand code, and it plots the score over time and keeps a photo history so the nurse can see at a glance whether a wound is improving or worsening.

3. Setup and access instructions

The app requires no account, login, or sign-up of any kind. On first launch, the app shows an empty patient list. Tap the "+" button to add a patient (only a name is required), open the patient, and tap the "+" button to add a DESIGN-R® record: select one option in each category and tap "Save" (the "Add photo" button lets you attach up to 4 photos from the camera or photo library; iOS will ask for camera/photo permission at that point). Adding a second record for the same patient displays the score-trend graph. No credentials or sample files are needed.

4. External services, tools, or platforms used

All patient records, DESIGN-R® evaluations, notes, and photos are stored only on the device (IndexedDB inside the app) and are never transmitted to any server. The only external service is GoatCounter (https://www.goatcounter.com/), a cookie-free, privacy-focused analytics service that receives an anonymous page-view event when the app is opened, used solely for the developer's own usage statistics. It receives no patient data, names, photos, or app content. The app does not use any authentication service, payment processor, data provider, advertising network, or AI service. This is disclosed in the app's Privacy Policy and in the App Privacy section (Usage Data > Product Interaction, used for Analytics, not linked to the user, not used for tracking).

5. Regional differences

The app functions identically in all regions. There are no region-specific features, content, or restrictions. The UI is in Japanese because DESIGN-R® is a Japanese clinical standard and the target users are Japanese healthcare workers, but behavior does not otherwise vary by region.

6. Regulated industry / protected material

JOKUSO+ is not a regulated medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or medical advice. It is a documentation and visualization tool: the nurse using the app makes their own clinical assessment according to the DESIGN-R® criteria, and the app only records the selected values, computes the standard total score, and displays the trend over time. This is stated in the app's Privacy Policy and App Store description, and the medical device declaration in App Store Connect is answered accordingly. The app does not contain or reference any protected third-party material, and no credentials for a regulated industry are applicable.
